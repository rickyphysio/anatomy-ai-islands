How to Play & Explore

Welcome to Ricky's anatomy learning island!

  • 3D Navigation: Drag to orbit, scroll/pinch to zoom, click any island or label to walk your adventurer across the suspension bridges and open the 3D Muscle Compendium.
  • RPG Rules & EXP: First island visit +5 EXP, first time answering a quiz question right +10 EXP, repeat correct answer +2 EXP, first island clear (2/3 correct) +10 EXP + 1 Stamp.
  • Central Citadel Final Boss: Earn 4+ Regional Stamps to unlock the 5-question Final Challenge on the Central Island (4/5 to pass & claim your downloadable/printable Certificate).
  • Living World & Fauna: Click strolling Island Scholars, Couriers, 9 animal species (🐿️🐰🦆🦋🐧🐬🐱🐋🕊️), or the 🐢 Sea-Turtle Shuttle to chat & trade 🐚 Calcium Shells!
  • Illustrative Notes (示意標示): All 157 muscles & quiz facts come directly from Ricky's Physiotherapy Anatomy Muscle Database CSV; original NPC/Monster names, 20 SVG diagrams for muscles without photos, and trade relics are marked 示意 (Illustrative).
